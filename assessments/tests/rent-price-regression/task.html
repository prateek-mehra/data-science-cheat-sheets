Overview

Harbor Homes lists apartments for rent across four boroughs. Landlords type their listings by hand, so the data is messy. The pricing team wants a model that suggests a monthly rent for new listings.

Tasks 1–3 are graded from the variables you leave in the notebook (read from the live kernel when you submit). Task 4 is graded from submission.csv.

Files

  • listings_train.csv — listings with the label rent (USD per month).
  • listings_test.csv — the same columns without rent.
  • neighbourhoods.csv — neighbourhood, borough, median_income, transit_score for all 24 neighbourhoods.

Task 1 · Clean the listings 15 pts

Load the training listings. Convert sqft to an integer, bedrooms to an integer (Studio = 0) and floor to an integer (G = 0). Some rents are data-entry typos: keep only listings with 300 ≤ rent ≤ 50,000. Store, on the cleaned training data:

  • task1_n_rows — the number of listings kept (int).
  • task1_median_rent_by_bedrooms — dict of bedrooms (0–4) → median rent, rounded to 1 decimal.
  • task1_median_rent_per_sqft — the median of rent / sqft across listings, rounded to 3 decimals.

Task 2 · Amenities 10 pts

Split amenities into individual amenities, lower-cased and stripped (a listing lists each amenity at most once). Using the cleaned training data, store:

  • task2_amenity_counts — dict of amenity → the number of listings that have it.
  • task2_doorman_premium — median rent of listings with a doorman minus median rent of listings without, rounded to 1 decimal.

Task 3 · Neighbourhoods 15 pts

Clean neighbourhood (strip spaces, Title Case) and join neighbourhoods.csv. Every listing should match. Store:

  • task3_median_rent_by_borough — dict of borough → median rent, rounded to 1 decimal.
  • task3_corr_income_rent — the Pearson correlation between median_income and rent across training listings, rounded to 3 decimals.

Task 4 · Predict rent 60 pts

Train a regression model and predict rent for every listing in listings_test.csv (it contains no typos). Save submission.csv:

listing_id,rent
123456,2450.0
654321,3810.5
...

Scoring: RMSE against the hidden rents. 0 points at RMSE ≥ 620, full points at RMSE ≤ 330, linear in between. Report a validation RMSE in your notebook. Rents are right-skewed, so consider modelling log(rent).
