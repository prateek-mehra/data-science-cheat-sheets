Overview

Helix Software sells a project-management app and, to some customers, on-site hardware. Every support ticket is triaged by hand to one of four teams: billing, technical, account or shipping. You'll analyse the ticket log and build a model that routes new tickets automatically.

Tasks 1–3 are graded from the variables you leave in the notebook (read from the live kernel when you submit). Task 4 is graded from submission.csv.

Files

  • tickets_train.csv — tickets with the label team.
  • tickets_test.csv — the same columns without team.
  • customers.csv — customer_id, plan, country, signup_date, has_hardware. plan is free, pro or enterprise.

Task 1 · Clean the tickets 15 pts

Load the training tickets. Drop tickets whose subject is missing or contains only whitespace. Map priority to exactly high, medium or low (case-insensitive: P1 = high, P2 and med = medium, P3 = low). Store, on the cleaned training data:

  • task1_n_rows — the number of tickets (int).
  • task1_priority_counts — dict of priority → count.
  • task1_team_share — dict of team → share of tickets, rounded to 3 decimals.

Task 2 · Response times 15 pts

Parse both timestamps. The response time is the hours between created_at and first_response_at (fractional hours, e.g. 90 minutes = 1.5). Using the cleaned training tickets, store:

  • task2_median_response_hours_by_team — dict of team → median response time in hours, ignoring unanswered tickets, rounded to 2 decimals.
  • task2_pct_unanswered — the share of tickets with no first response, rounded to 4 decimals.

Task 3 · Customer plans 15 pts

Join customers.csv onto the cleaned training tickets. Store:

  • task3_top_team_by_plan — dict of plan → the team that receives the most tickets from customers on that plan.
  • task3_enterprise_high_share — the share of enterprise-plan tickets with priority high, rounded to 4 decimals.

Task 4 · Route the test tickets 55 pts

Train a classifier and predict team for every ticket in tickets_test.csv. The subject line is the strongest signal, so you'll want to turn text into features (e.g. TfidfVectorizer or CountVectorizer); the metadata and the customer's plan help too. Save submission.csv:

ticket_id,team
TK123456,billing
TK654321,technical
...

Scoring: macro-averaged F1 over the four teams (each team counts equally, so the small shipping class matters). 0 points at macro F1 ≤ 0.55, full points at ≥ 0.90, linear in between. Report a validation score in your notebook.
