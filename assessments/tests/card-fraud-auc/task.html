Overview

Northwind Pay issues debit, credit and prepaid cards. The fraud team reviews a fixed number of flagged transactions a day, so what matters is ranking: fraudulent transactions should get higher scores than legitimate ones. Fraud is rare, which makes accuracy a useless measure here.

Tasks 1–2 are graded from the variables you leave in the notebook (read from the live kernel when you submit). Task 3 is graded from submission.csv.

Files

  • transactions_train.csv — January to April 2026, with the label is_fraud (1 = confirmed fraud).
  • transactions_test.csv — May 2026, same columns without is_fraud.
  • cards.csv — one row per card: card_id, card_type, card_country, issue_date, credit_limit (credit_limit is empty for debit and prepaid cards).

Task 1 · Clean and profile 15 pts

Load the training transactions. Convert amount to a float and normalise merchant_category to lower case with no surrounding spaces. Store:

  • task1_fraud_rate — the share of training transactions that are fraud, rounded to 4 decimals.
  • task1_n_cards — the number of distinct cards in the training data (int).
  • task1_max_amount — the largest amount, rounded to 2 decimals.

Task 2 · Where fraud concentrates 20 pts

Join cards.csv onto the training transactions. A transaction is foreign when its country is known and differs from the card's card_country (a missing country is not foreign). Store:

  • task2_fraud_rate_by_channel — dict of channel → fraud rate, rounded to 4 decimals.
  • task2_top_category — among cleaned merchant categories with at least 500 training transactions, the one with the highest fraud rate (a string).
  • task2_foreign_fraud_rate — the fraud rate of foreign transactions, rounded to 4 decimals.

Task 3 · Score May's transactions 65 pts

Train a model on the training data and predict, for every row in transactions_test.csv, the probability that it is fraud. Save submission.csv with exactly these columns and one row per test transaction:

tx_id,fraud_probability
T1234567,0.0123
T7654321,0.8810
...

Values must be between 0 and 1: use predict_proba(...)[:, 1], not predict.

Scoring: ROC AUC against the hidden labels. 0 points at AUC ≤ 0.80, full points at AUC ≥ 0.90, linear in between. Think about features that describe the card's behaviour, not only the single transaction, and validate in a way that respects time (train on earlier months, check on a later one).
