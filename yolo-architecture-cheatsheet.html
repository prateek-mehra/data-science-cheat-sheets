1. The detector in three parts

StageJob
BackboneExtract visual features at several resolutions (coarse to fine).
NeckFuse feature maps so small and large objects can use context across scales; commonly FPN + PAN.
HeadPredict box coordinates and class scores; may be anchor-based or anchor-free, coupled or decoupled.
Post-processFilter boxes, traditionally with NMS. End-to-end designs can learn one prediction per object and skip NMS.

2. Fast vocabulary

TermMeaning
AnchorPredefined box shape used as a starting point for box prediction.
FPN / PANTop-down and bottom-up paths that combine features at multiple scales.
CSP / C3 / C2fFeature-processing blocks that split and recombine paths to balance reuse and compute.
SPP / SPPFPooling modules that add wider spatial context; SPPF is a faster sequential variant.
DFLDistribution Focal Loss: predicts a distribution over box-edge distances for refined localization.
NMSNon-Maximum Suppression: removes overlapping duplicate detections after prediction.

3. What changed by generation?

VersionNew / importantRemoved / replaced
v1 · 2015Single network predicts boxes and classes from a whole-image grid.Separate region proposals and crop-by-crop classification.
v2 · 2016Anchors, batch norm, multi-scale training, passthrough features; YOLO9000 joint training.Fixed box-shape assumptions give way to learned anchor priors.
v3 · 2018Darknet-53 residual backbone; predictions at three scales improve size coverage.Single coarse output scale.
v4 · 2020CSPDarknet-53, SPP, PAN-style feature fusion; Mosaic and other training improvements.Kept a YOLOv3-style anchored head; gains came from backbone, neck, and training recipe.
v5 · 2020Ultralytics PyTorch implementation, C3 blocks, SPPF, efficient scaling.SPP pooling streamlined; anchor-based head remained in the classic design.
v6 · 2022Meituan's EfficientRep backbone, Rep-PAN neck, re-parameterized deployment graph.Training-time branches fold into simple inference-time convolutions.
v7 · 2022E-ELAN gradient-path aggregation and trainable bag-of-freebies.Training graph can simplify for inference.
v8 · 2023Ultralytics C2f backbone, anchor-free decoupled head, DFL box regression.Hand-designed anchors and shared box/class prediction head.
v9 · 2024GELAN aggregation and PGI gradient guidance preserve useful training information.Training-only guidance does not add matching inference overhead.
v10 · 2024Tsinghua dual assignment trains one-to-many and one-to-one prediction paths.Conventional NMS can be removed for end-to-end inference.
v11 · 2024Ultralytics C3k2 blocks and C2PSA attention refine efficient feature extraction.Backbone internals updated; familiar multi-scale detection structure remains.
v26 · 2026Ultralytics native end-to-end NMS-free inference, SPPF residual path, ProgLoss + STAL training recipe.DFL removed from head; simpler box regression and export path.
Lineage note: YOLOv5/8/11/26 are Ultralytics releases; YOLOv6, v7, v9, and v10 are distinct research branches. Version numbers do not imply a single uninterrupted author team or codebase.

4. The broad trend

  • Backbone: Darknet → CSP-based C3/C2f → C3k2 with attention.
  • Feature fusion: multi-scale prediction → FPN/PAN; SPP becomes faster SPPF.
  • Head: grid / anchor-based → anchor-free and decoupled.
  • Inference: NMS-based → learned end-to-end NMS-free variants.
  • Optimization: better training recipes and deployment-friendly graphs matter alongside layers.
